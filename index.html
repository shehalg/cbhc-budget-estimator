Home Care
Care Partner tool
For internal use only. This tool estimates what a participant's budget could look like. It is not a quote or an offer of service, and it does not replace the budget in TurnPoint.

Support at Home budget estimator

Build a participant's service plan on CBH rates and see how long their quarterly funding lasts, before anything goes into the Ankoora roster.

?What you'll need before you start
Have these open so the estimate matches the participant's file.
  • Notice of Decision for the classification level.
  • Support Plan for the approved service categories.
  • Co-contribution notice from Services Australia for contribution percentages. Do not guess them.
  • TurnPoint for the live budget, what has been spent, and weeks left in the quarter.
Rates are from the CBH Support at Home Pricing Schedule, from 1 September 2026, and exclude GST. The hours loaded are an example plan only.

Step 1: Funding level

Pick the participant's classification from their Notice of Decision. The government sets aside 10% for care management first. The rest is for services.

Government-funded spend from TurnPoint.

Step 2: Care categories

Tick the categories in the Support Plan. Set the contribution from the co-contribution notice.

?Assistive technology, restorative care or end-of-life funding?
These are separate one-off allocations and are not part of this estimate. Check the AT-HM workflow before ordering anything, and talk to the RN Quality Lead about restorative or end-of-life pathways.

Step 3: Select services

Pick a category tab, then enter hours per week in each rate band. The CBH rate is under each box. Most selected services have a two-hour minimum booking, so check the pricing schedule.

Step 4: Review

Check the summary beside this screen, then copy it into the care note. You can also compare the plan with what is rostered in Ankoora.

Enter what is currently rostered, before contribution. The summary will say if it differs from this plan.

Check before relying on this

  • Classification amounts are the annual figures on the Benetas estimator. Check them against the current Support at Home Program Manual (v4.4, September 2026).
  • Contribution ranges (Independence 5–50%, Everyday Living 17.5–80%) need the same check.
  • Personal care is shown at 0% contribution because Program Manual v4.3 says it is fully government-funded from 1 October 2026. CBH's schedule lists it under Independence services.
  • "Tight" means under 5% of the remaining budget is left. That is a guide for the conversation, not CBH policy.
